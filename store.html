No free tier. No trial. Every price below is the price the licence issuer charges - this page is generated from the same catalogue the issuer reads, so the number you read is the number you pay. Pay with PayPal, paste your transaction ID, the key is issued against it.

How activation works
  1. Pay. The button opens PayPal with the exact amount already filled in.
  2. Copy the transaction ID from your PayPal receipt.
  3. Paste it into the app's licence screen - or send it to the seller and the key comes straight back.
  4. Renew any time: pay again and paste the new transaction ID. The extra time is added to what you have left. You never lose a day you paid for, and your licence id never changes.
The issuer is published at https://license.ghvst.dev.
