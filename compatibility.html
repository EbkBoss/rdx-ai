TESTING AND COMPATIBILITY

Evidence, not compatibility theatre.

What each product supports, what was actually measured, and what remains limited. No claim of universal hardware, provider or permission support.

Shared release checks
  • 31 subscription options and distinct hosted checkout IDs verified.
  • 75 commerce-manifest assertions pass, including wrong prices, wrong QR payloads and missing artifacts.
  • 49 issuer sandbox assertions pass: payment verification, one key per payment, device binding, renewal and exact-byte tickets.
  • Every customer APK release stays DRAFT; delivery is issuer-side behind a signed paid ticket.
